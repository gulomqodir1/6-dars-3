The A-Z of what you need to build the future of green energy apps

A fully renewable energy system is dependent on flexible demand that can adapt to the intermittent supply from wind and solar. To create this flexibility, we need to connect and control the fast-growing demand from electric vehicles, heat pumps, and other energy hardware.

Connect API handles the complex task of connecting to and controlling all energy hardware OEMs across electric vehicles, thermostats, solar inverters, and home batteries.

Optimize API features, including smart EV charging, help your users consume energy in an innovative, more sustainable way.

Aggregate API lets you control and monetize all of your users' energy hardware as a single virtual power plant.

That way, you can focus on building the best green energy apps the world has seen.

Aggregate

Accelerate the transition to a sustainable future

Aggregation of flexible demand is key to a grid powered by renewable energy. By aggregating your customer's energy loads you can smooth out intermittencies caused by renewables. It also provides new revenue streams for your business.
